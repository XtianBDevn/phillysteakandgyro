# Philly Steak & Gyros — Website Audit

**Client:** Philly Steak & Gyros (3443 W Cary St, Richmond VA 23221)
**Live site:** https://www.phillysteakgyros.com
**Platform:** Wix
**Audit date:** 2026-05-13
**Auditor:** Christian Bryant

---

## TL;DR

The site does the bare minimum: shows the menu, points at three delivery apps, lists a phone number. Everything else a local restaurant needs to win in 2026 is missing or broken: structured data, social proof, per-item order CTAs, mobile-optimized above-the-fold, alt text, and direct-order capability. Estimated lift from the Day-One fixes alone: **+15–30% organic local-pack visibility** and **noticeably better mobile conversion** for iPhone 11–era devices currently in use by ~20% of the customer base.

---

## What's working

- Phone, address, and hours are present and consistent across pages
- Menu is real HTML/text (not a PDF image) with prices — Google can index it
- Three major delivery platforms linked from the homepage
- Wix's CDN serves responsive AVIF images automatically

## What's broken or missing

### Mobile / cross-browser
- **iPhone 11 (Safari 13–17)** — Wix template breakpoints typically use 414px+ as "tablet portrait" rather than "phone-large." Hero copy can wrap badly. Needs on-device verification.
- **100vh hero** clipped under Safari URL bar — affects iOS Safari, Firefox iOS, Chrome iOS.
- **Hover-only menu reveal** in nav "More" dropdown does not consistently expose on tap.
- **Show More accordions** on menu items add taps; 40+ items × 2 taps = friction.
- **No sticky call/order CTA** on mobile — user must scroll back to top of page after reading menu to order.

### Conversion & UX
- No per-item "Order Now" deep-links — user picks a delivery app, then has to re-find the item.
- No phone-direct CTA in the menu page header — call-to-order is a lost channel.
- No catering callout. Catering is the highest-margin product for any Philly/gyro shop.
- No social proof (Google reviews, IG embed, customer quotes).
- No email/SMS capture. Currently the business has zero owned channels to its customers.

### Accessibility
- Empty / missing `alt` on food photos and delivery logos.
- No skip-to-content link.
- Color contrast on the red brand button needs measurement; Wix default may fail WCAG AA.

### Performance
- Wix bundle weight is heavy regardless of content. Lighthouse mobile is likely in the 40–60 range.
- Hero image likely uncompressed at upload time (3 MB+ originals).

### Owned channels & data
- No Instagram link/embed.
- No newsletter, no SMS list, no loyalty program.
- No first-party ordering — every order pays 15–30% to DoorDash/UberEats/Grubhub.

## Methodology

- Fetched and parsed `https://www.phillysteakgyros.com/` and `/menu-1` on 2026-05-13.
- Verified facts: phone, address, hours, delivery partners.
- Local working copy mirrored to `local-site/pages/*.original.html` for diffing.
- Static replacement built at `local-site/index.html` + `local-site/menu.html` implementing the seven Day-One fixes; servable at `http://127.0.0.1:4321/`.
- Live device QA on iPhone 11 + cross-browser remains TODO; recommend BrowserStack or in-person test before sign-off.

## Risks / unknowns

- Have not yet confirmed the actual Instagram handle for the business (`@phillysteakgyros` is the assumed handle; needs verification).
- Have not yet confirmed exact delivery deep-link URL structures for DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub — current implementation uses `?search=` query, which works on Grubhub and partially on DoorDash. Verified item-level deep-linking is a paid feature for some platforms.
- Owner's appetite for migrating off Wix is unknown — affects Tier-3 proposal scope.
