**Goal:** Stop paying 30% to delivery apps for in-house customers. Build a customer database.

| Deliverable | Notes |
|---|---|
| Direct online ordering | Toast, Square Online, ChowNow, or Owner.com. Pick based on POS. |
| SMS loyalty / birthday club | Twilio + Airtable, or Cloutly / Toast Marketing |
| Catering landing page + lead form | High-margin product, currently invisible |
| Photography refresh | Half-day shoot, ~30 hero-quality dish photos |
| Reputation pipeline | Post-order SMS with GBP review link |
| Email newsletter setup | Klaviyo or Mailchimp, first 3 templates |

**Suggested price:** $4,500–$7,500 fixed + $X/mo recurring for SMS/email tooling.

**Outcome metrics (measure at day 60):**
- ≥ 5% of orders moving from third-party to direct (saves ~25% commission on every order)
- 500+ phone numbers in SMS list within 90 days
- 30+ new Google reviews (3.5× current rate)

**Goal:** Own the stack. Rank in the local pack. Scale paid acquisition.

| Deliverable | Notes |

**Outcome metrics (measure at day 120):**
- Page-1 ranking for "cheesesteak Richmond VA," "gyro Carytown"

## What's NOT in scope

- POS system migration (recommend Toast or Square but don't manage).
- Catering operations / fulfillment (we build the page, owner operates).

- Hiring / staffing.

## Decision points for the client

1. **Stay on Wix or migrate?** Affects Tier 3 entirely.
2. **Direct-ordering provider?** Toast vs. Square vs. ChowNow vs. Owner.com — depends on POS.
3. **Photography budget?** Either we shoot, or we use what they have. Big quality delta.
4. **Paid media budget?** Pilot at $500/mo or commit to $1,500/mo for faster signal.
